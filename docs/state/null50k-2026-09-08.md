## The fixture is a real null with real relatedness, and step 1 recovers it

`y_null = poly + cov + noise` (`harness/sim/02_build_dataset.py:601`): a
pedigree-TRANSMITTED polygenic background, **zero chr20 heritability**. So every
tested marker and gene has a true effect of exactly zero while the relatedness
the sparse GRM models is genuine. Quantitative, inverse-normalised, N = 50,000,
1,875 families of 4 among 42,500 singletons, pedigree-truth sparse GRM, 210
genes, `--is_fastTest=TRUE --is_single_in_groupTest=TRUE`.

**Positive control, from the fixture's own truth.** Planted `var(poly) = 0.353`
with `var_covar = 0.10`, so the true GRM share on the covariate-adjusted scale is
`0.353 / (0.353 + 0.55) = 0.391`. Step 1 fitted `theta = (0.5451, 0.3540)`, i.e.
**0.3937 -- 0.7 % relative**. The estimator finds a variance component that is
really there on this exact fixture, so the null result below is not the trivial
"a method that finds nothing".

That `theta` also makes this #106's worst case, as its own WHAT cell says:
`Sigma` has diagonal 0.899 and sib off-diagonal 0.177, ratio **0.197**, against
`tau1 x kinship / (tau0/W) ~ 0.16/11 = 0.015` on the bundled binary fixture.

## The null result: calibrated in the body, conservative in the tail

**`lambda_GC` is not usable on the SKAT-O columns here.** They carry a point mass
at `p == 1` exactly -- 26.7 % of the 210 Cauchy rows, 4.9 % of the 3,128 cells
(LEDGER #86, the SKAT-O exact-1 Cauchy fallback, DECIDED and deliberately left
in). Excluding the atom keeps only the small-p side and reports lambda 1.47 on
the Cauchy rows; including it maps the atom to `chi2 = 0` and reports 0.73. Both
numbers are artefacts of the atom -- the same shape as the binary ER atom that
`harness/check_null.R` warns about, in a column nobody had checked for it. The
evidence is the ECDF at the thresholds a rare-variant scan is used at, which a
point mass at the TOP cannot bias.

**Rows are not independent**: `max_MAF` (1e-4/1e-3/1e-2) is nested and the
annotations `high`, `high;medium`, `high;medium;low` are nested. The primary sets
are therefore one row per gene.

| set (HEAD) | n | P(p<=0.05) | P(p<=0.01) | min p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cauchy omnibus `Pvalue` | 210 | 0.029 | 0.000 | 1.96e-02 |
| `high;medium;low` / 0.01 `Pvalue` | 210 | 0.024 | 0.000 | 1.45e-02 |
| `high;medium;low` / 0.01 `Pvalue_Burden` | 210 | 0.043 | 0.000 | 1.39e-02 |
| `high;medium;low` / 0.01 `Pvalue_SKAT` | 210 | 0.038 | 0.005 | 8.04e-03 |
| all 3,128 cells `Pvalue` | 3128 | 0.051 | 0.0058 | 3.55e-03 |

| 5,923 REAL single variants | 5923 | 0.0496 | 0.0096 | 1.27e-05 |

The single-variant path is textbook: obs/exp 1.01 / 1.02 / 1.02 / 0.99 / 0.96
across alpha = 0.5, 0.2, 0.1, 0.05, 0.01, lambda 1.037, KS D = 0.012 p = 0.33 on
5,923 markers; min p 1.27e-05 against a 0.001/n floor of 1.7e-07. The region path
is at nominal down to alpha = 0.05 and CONSERVATIVE at alpha = 0.01 (obs/exp
0.42-0.64 on the three columns). Across 225 binomial intervals two came out
marginally low, both at alpha = 0.2 and both in nested strata whose intervals are
too narrow by construction. **No anti-conservatism anywhere.**

Two things `.singleAssoc.txt` needs splitting on, or the numbers are wrong: it
mixes real markers with the ultra-rare COLLAPSED pseudo-variants
(`<GENE>:<anno>:<max_MAF>`, 3,126 of the 9,049 rows), and those repeat the
identical p-value across the three nested `max_MAF` thresholds.

## What the fixes did, decomposed -- and the baseline the handoff named is confounded

**The 2026-09-01 `step2_group_y_null.txt` is NOT a clean BEFORE arm.** About 80
commits sit between it and HEAD. It shows up immediately: HEAD's
`.singleAssoc.txt` has **255 more rows** than the 2026-09-01 one, in six whole
gene blocks, and those six genes are exactly the six that escalate under
`--is_fastTest`. That is **LEDGER #50**, fixed `6b857ff5` on 2026-09-03. Diffing
against that file measures a week of work, not the four fixes.

So two more arms were run on the same fixture and the same frozen step-1 model:
`cca6b497` (#103's parent, freshly installed to
`harness/work/null50k-20260908/pre103/lib`) and `harness/work/lib-old` (#103
applied, #104/#105/#106 not). Between `cca6b497` and HEAD the only other src/R
commits are #91 (step 1 only, and step 1 is not re-run) and two doc-only ones, so
the arms isolate the fixes exactly. **#104/#105 provably cannot fire here**:
`R/SAIGE_SPATest_Region.R:363` gates `get_newPhi_scaleFactor_traitType` behind
`traitType == "binary"`.

Two exact identities make the decomposition readable off the diff. With `A =
tr(Phi)`, `B = 1'Phi 1` and the Score unchanged,
`BETAratio / SEratio^2 == B_old/A_old == kappa_w` (the #103 factor) and
`(Z_old/Z_new)^2 == B_new/B_old` (the burden-variance factor).

**#103 alone (`cca6b497` -> `690894f1`)** -- exactly as pre-registered:

* **ZERO p-values moved.** All three region columns on all 3,128 cells and all
  9,049 `.singleAssoc.txt` rows byte-identical. `c == 1` exactly.
* `BETA_Burden`/`SE_Burden` moved on exactly the **1,644 multi-marker cells** and
  on **none of the 1,484 single-marker cells**, where `Phi` is 1x1 and
  `kappa_w == 1` by construction. That is the built-in negative control, and it
  passing also proves the Score is untouched, which is what the identities assume.
* `kappa_w`: median **0.99928**, p01 0.979, p99 1.502, max **4.826**. Mostly just
  below 1 -- two rare variants rarely sit on the same haplotype, so `rbar` is
  slightly NEGATIVE for rare variants and `kappa_w = 1 + (m-1) rbar` dips below
  one -- with a tail of strongly positively-correlated cells. `1/4.826 = 0.2072`
  is exactly the minimum `BETA_Burden` ratio observed. The bundled goldens only
  ever showed 1.0013-1.0367; at 50k the correction reaches **-79 %**.

**#106 alone (`690894f1` -> HEAD)** -- direction as predicted, magnitude at the
low end of the predicted range:

* burden variance factor `c`: median **0.9988**, min **0.9387** on multi-marker
  cells (0.9421 on single-marker ones). The variance **FELL**, so the old run was
  CONSERVATIVE, which is the sign #106's own 50k binary measurement implies.
* `p` moved on 1,839-1,971 of 3,128 cells and got **SMALLER on 91.0 %, 91.0 % and
  93.3 %** of the moved `Pvalue` / `Pvalue_Burden` / `Pvalue_SKAT` rows; largest
  single move **-14.1 %**. On the markers: `var` down to **-7.5 %** (real MAC
  5-19 median -0.6 %), `p` smaller on **88.2 %** of the 2,867 that moved, largest
  **-20.2 %**. Real MAC >= 20 markers are almost untouched (median ratio 1.000):
  they take the common variance-ratio arm, which never enters the sparse solve.
* `kappa_w` is ~1 through this arm (median 1.000002, max 1.062) -- #106 changes
  `Phi` element-wise, so trace and sum move together but not identically.

**Calibration before vs after: unchanged, and that was predicted.** A ~0.1 %
median shift in the variance moves `lambda_GC` by ~0.005 against a sampling SE of
~0.08 on 210 genes. Measured, `cca6b497` -> HEAD: Cauchy 0.721 -> 0.726, hml/0.01
`Pvalue` 1.177 -> 1.178, `Pvalue_SKAT` 1.075 -> 1.079, all-cells `Pvalue`
1.023 -> 1.025, real markers 1.034 -> 1.037. **Every one moves UP, none moves
far** -- the pre-registered direction, at the pre-registered (undetectable)
magnitude. `P(p <= 0.05)` and `P(p <= 0.01)` are identical to four decimals on
every region set. The fixes are real and correctly signed; they are
calibration-neutral at this scale because the null was already conservative and
only 15 % of these samples have any off-diagonal at all.

## What this does and does not license

* It DOES retire "the fixes moved p-values by up to 12.6 %/23 % and nobody
  measured a null": at N = 50,000 with genuine relatedness, on a quantitative
  trait fitted with a sparse GRM -- the regime the coverage audit found had zero
  golden coverage -- the null is calibrated at alpha = 0.05, conservative at
  alpha = 0.01, and the fixes move it very slightly toward nominal.
* It does NOT test the BINARY SKAT-O path, where #104/#105 live and where the
  measured golden movement was 12.6 %. `y_binary` on this fixture is the arm that
  would, and it was not run here.
* It does NOT test power. The fixture's two known defects (wrong NCP model, no
  directional genes) still block the POWER controls; see the
  `saige-bench50k-fixture` memory.
