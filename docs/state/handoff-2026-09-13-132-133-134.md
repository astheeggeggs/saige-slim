# LEDGER #134, #132 and #133 fixed (2026-09-13)

Three rows of the pseudo-marker / weight-commensurability family, one commit
each, each gated at `both` modes. The ledger went from 13 open to **9**.

| commit | row | what moved |
|---|---|---|
| `a2962701` | #134 | the band reads an allele count, not a weighted sum -- 15 files |
| `58785e12` | #132 | ACAT-V's pseudo-marker weight is commensurable -- 6 files, 2 columns |
| `8bb5a510` | #133 | the pseudo-marker's MAC is its raw carrier-allele count -- 3 files |

Read the three prediction documents beside this one -- `prediction-134.md`,
`prediction-132.md`, `prediction-133.md`. Each was written BEFORE its gate and
then had its OUTCOME appended, including where the prediction was wrong.

## What the three rows were, in one line each

- **#134**: the collapsed pseudo-marker's variance-ratio band and sparse-GRM
  choice were decided by the WEIGHTED collapse sum, so the SCALE of a group
  file's weights chose the statistical treatment. They now read `MAC_nw`.
- **#132**: the same pseudo-marker's ACAT-V COMBINATION weight was a hardcoded
  1, not commensurable with the surviving markers' own weights, so ACAT-V
  depended on the weight scale. It is now the MEAN of the folded markers'
  non-zero weights, applied at the ACAT-V call site only.
- **#133**: its reported MAC was derived from a FOLDED frequency while its
  vector (never folded since #130) and its case/control halves were not, so
  `MAC != MAC_case + MAC_control` above alt frequency 0.5. The COUNTS are now
  raw; the MAF deliberately is not.

## The single most useful thing here: two rows' WHAT cells were wrong

**#133's stated defect and both of its candidate fixes were mis-located**, and
that is now recorded on the row so they are not re-opened:

- Candidate A (fold the case/control halves per stratum) is INCOHERENT on a
  mixed cell -- measured, it returns NEGATIVE values (-1064, -997) on four of six
  cells of `plink_group_urmixed_burden`, because the cell MAC is a sum of
  per-marker folded counts while the halves are raw cell-level sums.
- Candidate B (report the unfolded AC in MAC) was said to move `MAC` in every
  group golden. Only three goldens have any row where the identity fails.

**#134's row was wrong about its own measurement's reason.** It recorded that
doubling every weight moved no p-value "because the band boundaries were not
crossed by a factor of 2". They ARE crossed -- REVEL 6.38 -> 12.76 and
9.51 -> 19.02 both cross 10 -- but `selectVarianceRatio` maps `MAC <= 10` AND
`10 < MAC <= 20.5` both to `varRatio(0)`, so only 20.5 is LIVE. Right conclusion,
wrong reason, and the wrong reason would send a test author straight to x2, the
one factor that cannot detect the bug. The characterization uses **x0.25**.

## What each fix cost, and the traps inside them

### #134 -- two live sites, not three

`cm.isER` is `&&`-gated on `erAllowedVec[r]`, true only when every weight of the
set is exactly 1.0, and then the collapse weight is 1.0 so `MAC_nw == MAC`
exactly. The ER site is INERT and no test may assert it. The live sites are the
band and the sparse-GRM choice.

Only **6** of the "8 of 12 cells land in a different band" actually change the
value selected, for the same `varRatio(0)` reason above.

### #132 -- ONE VECTOR, TWO PURPOSES

`AnnoWeights` is not only ACAT-V's combination vector: `wStatVecList <-
lapply(AnnoWeightsList, function(w) StatVec * w)` and the weighted variance
sub-matrix are built from it, i.e. the BURDEN and SKAT statistics. There the
pseudo-marker's 1 is CORRECT, because C++ has already baked `w_r` into the
collapse `max(w_r*G)` (#17) -- weighting its score again double-counts it.

The first attempt wrote the mean into that shared vector and did exactly that.
`test-step2-multiweight-behaviour.R` caught it on the first run, failing on
`Pvalue_Burden`/`BETA_Burden`/`SE_Burden` rather than on anything to do with
ACAT-V. There are now two vectors: `AnnoWeightsList` for the statistics,
`AnnoWeightsACATVList` for the combination.

**The row's "normalise before ACAT-V" candidate is RETIRED AS INERT**:
`R/CCT_modified.R:52` already does `weights <- weights / sum(weights)`, so
pre-normalising leaves every relative weight unchanged. It would have shipped as
a fix that changed nothing.

### #133 -- the same shape again, and MAFVec is NOT a reported quantity

A first attempt made `MAF`/`MAF_nw` raw as well as the counts, on the premise
that MAF is "the reported MAF". **False, and the check is one grep**: nothing
writes `MAFVec` to any output, and its only consumers are the two `dbeta()` calls
in `R/SAIGE_SPATest_Region.R` (`:324`, `:430`). The pseudo-marker's only reported
frequency is `AF_Allele2` in the single-variant file, already raw.

So making MAF raw changed the WEIGHTING. Measured before it was reverted, and
kept as the #131 evidence: every p-value stayed byte-identical and BETA/SE held
its ratio at -0.210239, but `BETA_Burden` went from -0.860935 to
**-1.01393e+13**, because `dbeta(0.778,1,25)` = 5.1e-15. The shipped fix touches
the two counts only, and the BETA/SE columns holding still is the check that the
weighting did not leak.

## Test strength: one loss, recovered rather than accepted

#132 invalidated `test-invariants-goldens.R`'s first-principles recompute of
`Pvalue_ACATV`, which hardcoded the pseudo-marker's weight at 1. An interim
version solved for that weight and only bounded the root inside the set's own
weight range -- **much weaker**, because on the CADD set that range is 0.8 to 10,
so a wrong membership, a wrong per-marker p or a modestly shifted golden still
yields a root in range. It failed only on gross errors.

The lost information is already an output SAIGE can write.
`--is_output_markerList_in_groupTest=TRUE` emits a `.markerList.txt` naming the
surviving rare and folded ultra-rare rsIDs per (region, annotation, max_MAF).
`make_goldens.sh` now passes it on the six #132 movers and **the sidecars are
COMMITTED**, so folded membership is part of the pinned record -- which #118 and
#134 both wanted and never had. Predicted inert-except-additive and checked: six
new files, ZERO `DIFFERS` anywhere else.

The recompute is first-principles again with no free parameter, and the sidecar
being weight-INDEPENDENT is what makes it honest: the per-set `w != 0` filter is
applied by the TEST, which is the implementation's own rule
(`src/Main.cpp:3052`), not a restatement of its output. Drilled by planting over
the 12 mixed cells: correct mean 12/12, the old hardcoded 1 0/12, one member
dropped 0/12.

**A claim was withdrawn on the way.** "The solved weights are exact means,
confirming the implementation" was CIRCULAR -- both the weight and the value it
was solved from came from the same output. The non-circular confirmation is that
the mean of the CADD weights of the twelve markers the SIDECAR names is 6.525,
computed from the group file with no reference to any ACAT-V output.

## Standing decisions recorded today

- **The FlexRV collapse cutoff stays at 10; 0 is not to be recommended for a
  BINARY trait until a replicate null sweep shows type-I error holds.**
  SAIGE-GENE+ introduced the collapse FOR CALIBRATION, this repo has never
  measured the regime where that matters, and every calibration result here --
  null and power -- describes cutoff 10. In `CLAUDE.md`'s FlexRV section.
  **It constrains a live recommendation**: `flexrv-study-decisions-2026-09-12.md`
  recommends new arms "at collapse 0" and its arms include `y_binary`. That memo
  is dated and not edited; `next.md` names it and applies the constraint.
- **#131 gains the beta-vs-carrier-frequency question, with a number.** A beta
  set applies `dbeta(MAF, a, b)` to the pseudo-marker's CARRIER frequency, which
  block-down-weights a collapsed set below any of its own constituents: at
  N = 1,000 a single singleton gets 24.70 while a pseudo-marker of 2/5/10/20/50
  of them gets 24.41/23.54/22.17/19.64/**13.62**. Upstream SAIGE-GENE+'s rule,
  inherited with the beta path, NOT introduced by #133, and the sim study's null
  and power arms were both run under it. A decision only if someone wants the
  beta path to match the paper's weight-before-collapse arithmetic, which was
  argued against on blast-radius grounds.

## Method notes worth carrying

- **Predict from the committed bytes before the gate, and predict COLUMNS, not
  just files.** #132's sharpest check was "only `Pvalue_ACATV` and
  `Pvalue_ACATO` may move"; it is what proved the fix had not leaked into the
  statistics.
- **A golden-reading test proves nothing until you plant.** #133's and #132's
  characterizations were both drilled by planting the old behaviour and a wrong
  membership, not merely observed to pass.
- **Verify the ARTEFACT, not the exit code.** A scratch `R CMD INSTALL` failed
  with `Error 127` (no compiler on PATH when `src/*.o` had been cleaned), the
  scratch library was empty, `library(SAIGE)` silently fell back to the patched
  main library, and the drill reported a false green. Check for `SAIGE.so` and
  assert the loaded path.
- **A positional diff of two files that may differ in length is not a diff.**
  One cost a confident, wrong hypothesis: it reported 73% of rows differing and
  a max |dlog10 p| of 51.7 where the keyed comparison gives 12.0% and 5.77.
